You command four players. Drag from one of your players and drop: it runs there at full speed and stops. Shift-drop adds the destination after its current path (while you hold shift the preview starts from the path's last waypoint, highlighted); with shift the drag can start anywhere (away from your players it moves the selected one, and a shift-click queues the clicked spot). A plain click on a player stops it. The selected player's stance is set with keys 1/2/3/4 or the buttons; Q/W/E/R select players 1–4. Labels show number, stance (D/P/S/B) and how much of the ball is near that player. Bright = where the ball is likely to be. Bars at the goals show the probability of the ball being inside. On the pitch. Dot + arrowhead: a player and the way it faces. Shaded disc and circle ahead of a player: its catch zone, where its well pulls the ball and its kicks act (the circle is the well's width, 1 sigma; its size and distance grow with the reach slider). Solid circle: Defense stance (wide zone, farther ahead). Dashed circle: Pass, Shot or Dribble. Thick circle: your selected player. Thin dotted lines in team colour ending in a small ring: where the player's kick goes (Pass: one line per ally, fainter = less of the ball sent there; Shot: the goal; Defense: straight ahead). White dotted line with dots: the path you gave one of your players and its waypoints.
