You command four players. Click or drag from one of your players: the drag is its velocity vector (1 m of drag = 1 m/s), a plain click stops it. The selected player's stance is set with keys 1/2/3 or the buttons; Q/W/E/R select players 1–4. Labels show number, stance (A/D/P) and how much of the ball is near that player. Bright = where the ball is likely to be. Bars at the goals show the probability of the ball being inside.
